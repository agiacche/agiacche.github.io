We introduce metric Möbius graphs, extending ribbon graphs to the non-orientable setting, whose moduli space contains both Riemann surfaces and non-orientable Klein surfaces. Each metric Möbius graph carries a measure of its non-orientability. Counting lattice points in this moduli space, weighted by this measure, we establish a refined recursion extending Norbury's lattice point count. Taking a suitable limit yields a recursion for Euclidean volumes that refines the Witten–Kontsevich recursion. As an application, we give a geometric definition of the refined Euler characteristic and compute it explicitly, answering a question of Goulden, Harer, and Jackson.
