We construct an explicit closed string dual to any interacting Hermitian one-matrix model, away from the double-scaling limit. Matrix and string correlators agree to all orders in the genus expansion and all orders in the 't Hooft coupling. The dual worldsheet theory is a supersymmetric B-twisted Landau–Ginzburg model coupled to two-dimensional topological gravity. We give an explicit dictionary translating matrix traces into worldsheet vertex operators, turning matrix model correlators into computable integrals over the moduli space of Riemann surfaces, and we perform direct cross-checks validating the duality on both sides. This instantiates a gauge/string duality in the standard 't Hooft regime and gives a tractable worldsheet model for studying the AdS/CFT correspondence away from the free-field limit.
