We study equivariant Gromov–Witten invariants of Calabi–Yau fivefolds through the physical lens of M2-branes and supergravity. We propose a modular interpretation of M2-branes supported on several smooth curves meeting at an $$n$$-fold point, which produces conjectural formulas that we convert into closed-form expressions for quintuple Hodge integrals. We relate these conjectures to K-theoretic correspondences for local curves, and link the generating series of constant maps to Donaldson–Thomas theory. We prove the main conjectures in two special limits, and along the way obtain new closed formulas for certain triple Hodge integrals.
