We give a bijective proof of a recursive excision formula for maps of arbitrary topology, which yields topological recursion formulas for their enumeration at the level of generating series without any analyticity assumption. The construction extends to maps decorated with self-avoiding loop models and to stuffed maps, whose faces may carry arbitrary topology. A key feature is that it gives a combinatorial interpretation to every term appearing in the topological recursion formalism, including the previously unexplained recursion kernel. The bijection proceeds by iterating Tutte's algorithm across topology-changing moves, and can be understood as a pair-of-pants decomposition driven by a path issuing from the root of the first boundary, in parallel with the Mirzakhani–McShane identity for hyperbolic surfaces.
