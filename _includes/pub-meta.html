{% comment %} Renders the compact "authors · venue · year" metadata line for a publication. Shared by the publications list, the individual publication page, and the CV, so the phrasing only exists once. In "cv" mode specifically, the coauthors piece breaks onto its own line rather than being joined with " · " like everywhere else - the CV's compact, bulleted format reads as "Title, with coauthors" on one line and "Venue · Year · links" on the next (see the .pub-item--cv rules in _sass/layout/_academic.scss).
